# Lost Property Office
Nimbus Rail operates a single central Lost Property Office, located at Ashcombe Hub on Concourse B, open 08:00 to 18:00 every day of the week. Items found on any trainset or platform are logged and transferred to Ashcombe Hub within 48 hours.

Recovered items are retained for 60 days from the date they are logged. After 60 days, unclaimed items are donated to charity; unclaimed HaloPay-linked cards are securely destroyed instead. Passengers can reclaim an item in person at Concourse B with proof of ownership, or request courier delivery for a flat 12-credit handling fee.
