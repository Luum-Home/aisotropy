================================================================================
THE GOVERNING FINDING — Dify's OWN license (not a dependency): a HARD BLOCKER for
Orbit's stated use case.
================================================================================
Dify itself ships under a MODIFIED Apache-2.0 (repo-root LICENSE, verbatim §1a):

  "a. Multi-tenant service: Unless explicitly authorized by Dify in writing, you
   may not use the Dify source code to operate a multi-tenant environment.
     - Tenant Definition: Within the context of Dify, one tenant corresponds to
       one workspace."

Orbit IS a multi-tenant support chatbot (many customer workspaces behind one
service). Operating it on self-hosted Dify is precisely the prohibited use and
requires a COMMERCIAL license from LangGenius. Under aisotropy's
.claude/rules/cos/license-policy.md this source-available/commercial-use
restriction is a BLOCKER for a SaaS product — the same class as BSL/SSPL/ELv2.

  Net: the dependency TREE clears the hard-blocker gate (0 GPL/AGPL/BUSL/SSPL);
  Dify's OWN modified-Apache multi-tenant clause does NOT — it is the single most
  important license finding in this arena, and it is a governance finding, not a
  dependency one. aisotropy is 100% MIT/Apache with no such field-of-use limit.
